The Executive Circle is a membership level of People’s Empowerment PAC. Member dues support PEPAC’s work to expand economic opportunity and leadership in Black and underserved communities across North Carolina.

The Executive Circle is a private membership network designed for Black executives and senior professionals seeking greater visibility, stronger professional connections, and access to opportunities that often move through trusted networks first.

Our mission is simple

To close the leadership gap by growing the number of Black leaders in executive roles, on boards, and in the rooms where decisions about our communities are made.

Why The Executive Circle Exists

Many leadership and executive opportunities are filled long before they become public. Hiring decisions frequently happen through referrals, trusted relationships, and professional networks.

Many talented Black professionals remain outside of those circles despite having the experience and qualifications to lead. The Executive Circle exists to change that.

Through our growing network, members gain exposure to:

  • Executive recruiters
  • Hiring managers
  • Corporate decision-makers
  • Industry leaders
  • Board members
  • Founders & entrepreneurs
  • Senior professionals

Our goal is to help ensure more Black professionals are present in the rooms where opportunities begin.

Referral Program

Waitlist

To maintain a high-quality member experience, membership capacity may be limited. Qualified applicants may be placed on our waitlist and receive priority access to future openings, invitations to select events, community updates, and Founding Member pricing when available.

Membership Philosophy

Intentionally curated.

Our value comes from the quality of our members, the strength of our relationships, and the trust built within the community. Membership is not guaranteed — we carefully review every application.

Before you apply, ask yourself:

  • Am I serious about expanding my professional network and executive visibility?
  • Do I understand the value of investing in relationships that can influence long-term career growth?
  • Do I value access, trust, discretion, and professional excellence?
  • Am I prepared to contribute positively to the community?

If your answer is yes, we invite you to apply.
