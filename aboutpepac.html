Mission Statement

The People’s Empowerment PAC builds political and economic power for Black communities.

We fight systemic racism and injustice by empowering political candidates who are champions for equity and truly represent Black communities. We provide economic benefits that strengthen our communities. We mobilize grassroots support to challenge the status quo and demand real change.

Political Power

Champions for equity

We stand behind leaders who truly represent Black communities, so we have a real voice where decisions get made.

Economic Power

Benefits that strengthen us

Membership puts real savings in members’ pockets and puts Black-owned businesses in front of new customers.

Influence

Connecting the voices people trust

We organize influencers, thought leaders, and partner organizations, keep them informed on what’s at stake, and connect them so their combined reach becomes real influence.

Networks

Opening doors for each other

We build networks for business owners, executives, and professionals, connecting them to the relationships and opportunities that usually move behind closed doors.

Together

We are building a future where Black communities are empowered.
