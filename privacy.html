Last updated: October 6, 2026

Our commitment

People's Empowerment PAC maintains strict privacy policies, ensuring that personal information of our users and members is not sold, rented, released, or traded to others without prior consent or a legal obligation. Personal information includes name, email address, phone number, and other contact information.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us when you fill out a form on this website — for example, to volunteer, contact us, apply to The Executive Circle, or join B.O.S.S. This may include your name, email address, phone number, location, organization, and anything you write in a message.

Contributions are processed by ActBlue on ActBlue's secure website. We do not collect or store credit card or bank information on this website. Information you provide to ActBlue is governed by ActBlue's own privacy policy and is reported to us as required by campaign finance law.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your messages and requests
  • To coordinate volunteer, membership, and event activities
  • To send you updates, alerts, and other information about our work, including by email and — only if you consent — by text message
  • To comply with campaign finance reporting and other legal obligations

Text messaging

If you check the text-message consent box on one of our forms, we will use your mobile number to send the text messages described in our Terms & Conditions. Consent to receive text messages is not required to submit a form, make a contribution, or participate in our work. Mobile numbers and text-messaging opt-in information are not shared with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes.

SMS Opt-Out: If you are receiving text messages from us and wish to stop receiving them, simply respond with “STOP” to the number from which you received the message. Once we receive your message, you will no longer receive further text messages from us.

Service providers

We use trusted service providers to operate this website, deliver form submissions, process contributions, and send messages. They may handle your information only to provide those services to us.

Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and reliability.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@peoplesempowermentpac.org.
