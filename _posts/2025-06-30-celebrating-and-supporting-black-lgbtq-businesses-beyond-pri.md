Pride Month is a time for celebration, awareness, and unity in the LGBTQ+ community. But the end of June should not mark the end of our commitment. Supporting Black LGBTQ+ businesses requires sustained action throughout the entire year. Each purchase, story shared, and event attended is a step toward fostering a more inclusive economy. Here are some actionable ways to continue uplifting Black LGBTQ+ entrepreneurs after Pride Month.

## Shop Black, Shop LGBTQ+

The most impactful way to support Black LGBTQ+ businesses is by shopping from them regularly. During Pride Month, many companies showcase their commitment to LGBTQ+ rights, but visibility tends to fade afterward. Seek out local Black LGBTQ+ establishments, such as restaurants, boutiques, or salons. Statistics reveal that shopping locally can increase community revenue by over 40%, which significantly benefits local economies. Encourage your friends and family to join you in choosing where to spend your money.

## Promote Their Stories

Highlighting the journeys of Black LGBTQ+ business owners can boost their visibility and inspire others. Use your social media platforms or local groups to share profiles and interviews. For example, an Instagram story featuring a Black LGBTQ+ business owner can reach hundreds, if not thousands, of viewers. Not only does this celebrate their achievements, but it can also drive more traffic to their businesses. By amplifying their voices, you help create a ripple effect of inspiration and support.

## Collaborate and Support Events

Engaging with events hosted by Black LGBTQ+ entrepreneurs fosters connections and builds community. Attend local festivals, markets, or workshops to discover their work firsthand. For example, participating in a Pride festival can draw hundreds of attendees, showcasing small businesses to a larger audience. Offer to collaborate on marketing efforts or co-host events. Your involvement not only brings attention to their work, but it also allows you to develop lasting relationships within the community.

## Educate Yourself and Others

Understanding the unique challenges faced by Black LGBTQ+ entrepreneurs is vital. Take advantage of workshops, literature, and webinars that address issues such as access to funding or systemic barriers in business. Sharing this knowledge within your community helps foster a more informed dialogue. For instance, statistics indicate that only 1% of venture capital funding goes to Black entrepreneurs, highlighting the urgent need for education and advocacy. The more we know, the better we can advocate for change.

## Create a Long-term Commitment

Supporting Black LGBTQ+ businesses is not just a task for Pride Month; it requires ongoing commitment. Subscribe to newsletters, follow their social media, and continue to promote their work throughout the year. Aim to establish genuine relationships. If you regularly feature a particular business on social media, consider a monthly spotlight to keep their brand in the community's mind. This consistent support can increase brand loyalty and customer engagement.

## Making a Lasting Impact

Supporting Black LGBTQ+ businesses is about more than just acknowledging Pride Month; it's about fostering inclusivity in our society. By actively choosing to shop, share stories, participate in events, and educate ourselves, we can create meaningful change. Let's ensure our support for the Black LGBTQ+ community is a year-round effort—because every month deserves to honor their incredible contributions!
